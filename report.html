02

Derivation rules

All three clocks are the same kind of signal — 1-sample triggers, never held high. They differ only in how often they fire.

Pixel Clock

Trigger. Pulses once, one sample wide, every time the laser strobe reads 5. 256 pulses per frame — one per pixel counted.

Line Clock

Trigger, twice per line: once on the line's 1st pixel (line-start, bold marker), once on its 16th (line-complete, dotted marker — landing on that pixel's Pixel Clock pulse). 32 pulses per frame.

Frame Clock

Trigger, twice per frame: once on line 1's 1st pixel (frame-start, bold marker), once on line 16's 16th pixel (frame-complete, dotted marker). 2 pulses per frame.
